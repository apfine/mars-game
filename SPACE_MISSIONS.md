# Space mission model

The destination menu supports all eight Solar System planets, the Moon, Gargantua, and Miller's planet. Earth, Mars, the Moon, and Miller have local landing scenes. Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune use the same local force integrator: arrival is a circular coast at the profile gravity and radius, and the ship cannot land. That coast does not charge a separate capture burn, matching Earth and Mars arrival. Gargantua is not integrated with Newtonian gravity. The route planner inserts reachable staging legs for the ship's modeled injection propellant and sends Gargantua routes through the spherical wormhole near Saturn. The transit flies into the mouth, holds in the throat, then leaves in the other chart.

Local flight sums engine thrust, wind-relative drag, lift, and inverse-square gravity using the active profile. The local radial/tangent integrator conserves angular momentum to integration accuracy. Assisted orbit correction burns propellant. The trajectory overlay predicts a ballistic path using current gravity, atmosphere, attitude, and throttle; it cannot know future pilot inputs or guidance changes. Solar cruise follows a computed two-body Hohmann ellipse with automatic launch-window phasing. Alignment and the injection burn are shown before coast; the burn consumes fuel according to the rocket equation. Long cruise is time-compressed, then slows visually near encounter so the planet's true angular size grows on screen. At large distances, an optical point-source marker and target label make subpixel planets locatable without changing their physical render radius. Giant-planet capture, multi-body perturbations, gravity assists, and full transfer optimization are outside this model.

Starship propellant capacity is modeled as 1,200 tonnes. An orbital tanker offers 1,200 tonnes of deliverable propellant, with an animated approach, soft dock, hard dock, visible umbilical, and conserved transferred quantity. The displayed transfer is compressed 30-fold from a modeled 5 tonnes/second rate. The route planner checks that each injection leg is within a 94%-full ship tank with a 36-tonne correction reserve. It does not budget a destination capture burn. Transfer arrivals retain the propellant the injection burn left behind, including the final destination; entry and landing then spend that remaining mass.

Gargantua's story mass is 100 million solar masses. The Miller clock is modeled at 1 local hour to 7 distant years (61,362:1), confined to Miller's local worldline. Gargantua is a separate navigation waypoint: the game does not apply a Newtonian field at its horizon. The wormhole is a fictional traversable portal between coordinate charts. Its spherical destination view and the black hole's shadow, disk, and lensed arcs are visual approximations, not Kerr geodesic ray tracing. Miller's Earth-like radius, local gravity, air, and fixed landing raft are gameplay assumptions; the film does not establish those values. Its moving 900 m tidal crest recurs about once per local hour and is an artistic ocean model, not a hydrodynamics solution.

Sources: [Kip Thorne on Gargantua and Miller](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~kip/index.html/PubScans/VI-59.pdf); [Interstellar wormhole visualization paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03809); [black hole lensing paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03808); [JPL planetary physical parameters](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/phys_par.html).

The Saturn globe uses a single high-detail local mesh and smoothly varying cloud bands; the ring mesh has enough radial segments to avoid a polygonal edge. Its soft banding and layered rings are guided by [NASA's Saturn imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/saturn-wfc3-uvis/) and [Cassini's ring views](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/ringscape-color/).

Vehicle visual check: the existing Starship model is 9 m in diameter and about 125 m as a stacked system, consistent with SpaceX's published 9 m / 124.4 m overview. It already includes the ogive nose, windward tile field, four moving flaps, six upper-stage engines and 33 booster engines, so this pass retained that geometry.
